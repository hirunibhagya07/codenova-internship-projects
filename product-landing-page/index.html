A few little answers

Curious about something?

Here are a few things you might like to know before joining our imaginary club.

✿
What is the Petal & Paper Club?

It's a stationery subscription concept for this student project. Each bundle features a mix of paper goods, stickers and journaling extras.

What's inside each bundle?

Each tier includes a different collection of notebooks, stickers, washi tape, postcards or other stationery-inspired keepsakes. The exact contents would depend on the selected plan.

How does yearly billing work?

The yearly option shows a discounted equivalent monthly price. The full twelve-month amount is billed once for the year in this pricing example.

Can I place a real order here?

Not yet! This is a frontend demo. The buttons demonstrate the landing-page design, but a real store would need order management and a secure payment system.
